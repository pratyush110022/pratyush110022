## 👨‍💻 What I Work On

My work sits at the intersection of **GIS, data engineering, automation, and analytics**. I enjoy building practical workflows that collect, clean, connect, and explain data — especially when location is a key part of the problem.

- **Geospatial & location intelligence:** POI data, spatial workflows, mapping, routing, and location-based analysis.
- **Python automation & data pipelines:** browser automation, API integrations, data extraction, validation, deduplication, and structured exports.
- **GeoAI & applied AI:** conversational geospatial workflows, route-aware EV planning, and AI orchestration with deterministic spatial calculations.
- **Business & machine-learning analytics:** SQL analysis, Power BI reporting, customer segmentation, and text sentiment classification.

## 🔍 How I Approach Data Problems

**Discover → Extract → Validate → Standardize → Analyze → Communicate**

I focus on the complete workflow, not just the script: understand the source, design repeatable collection, handle missing or duplicate records, preserve useful attributes, and make the results usable through maps, datasets, or dashboards.
